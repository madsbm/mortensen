## What it did

A companion app for students that put the things you check every school day in one place:

- A lecture calendar.
- Homework and assignments.
- Absence.
- Borrowed library books.

## Adoption

I built it independently while I was still in high school. It reached **more than 1,000 users** before it was shut down.

## What it led to

Lectimate helped me land my current job at Side-Walk. Its source isn't public, but the lesson stuck: build something real people rely on every day, and keep it working.

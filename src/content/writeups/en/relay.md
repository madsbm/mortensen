## The idea

Relay is built around persistent memory. Instead of being briefed from scratch on every task, it keeps what it learns from past work, so it gets more useful over time.

## Task intake

Work doesn't only arrive in an editor. Relay takes in tasks from the places teams already use, such as email and Jira, with more sources to come.
